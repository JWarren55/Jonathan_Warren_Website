## Update the portfolio

- **Profile photo.** Add a photo as `images/jonathan-warren.jpg`, then follow the comment inside the portrait figure in `index.html` to display it. The frame crops the photo to a 5:6 portrait.
- **Item details.** Each project, job, and school has its own `article` in `index.html`. Add paragraphs or bullet points inside that article. Cards and entries grow with the content.
- **Project links.** Sugar Watch uses its existing app URL on the title, preview, and “Open Sugar Watch” link. For another project, wrap its title in an anchor with its URL when you have one.
